IFI · A 30-Day Staff Challenge

Be. Think. Do.

With Jesus. Three daily rhythms that help our ministry flow from our relationship with Him.

Begin Day One

Why we’re doing this

Our work for Him should flow from our time with Him.

At IFI, we spend our lives helping international students encounter Jesus, grow in their faith, and live out the gospel. But our ministry must flow from our own ongoing relationship with Him. For 30 consecutive days, we’ll build daily habits centered on Jesus, and grow in how we live, think, and serve, together as an IFI community.

Be with Jesus

15 MINUTES

Prioritize time in relationship with Him. Spend at least 15 minutes through prayer, Scripture, journaling, silence, solitude, worship, or other spiritual disciplines.

Think with Jesus

10 PAGES

Let Him shape your mind. Read at least 10 pages of nonfiction that grows your understanding of God, Scripture, the Christian life, or the world. You can also reflect on Scripture, memorize it, and remember His faithfulness.

Do with Jesus

BLESS 1 PERSON

Join Him in loving others. Encourage a coworker, pray with someone, serve selflessly, have an intentional conversation about Jesus, practice hospitality, or share what He’s been teaching you.

Our goal is formation, not performance. This isn’t about earning God’s favor, competing with colleagues, or checking boxes.
